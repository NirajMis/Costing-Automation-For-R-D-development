Open Questions — Pending Business Sign-off

4 open
1

Category taxonomy. BRP27's manual sheet used Housing / L2 / Driver / SPD / Consumable / Hardware / Wiring — a different set than the documented Hardware/Packing/Housing/Electronics/Consumables/Wires/Labour.

Impact: changes which bucket every BOM line lands in.
2

AI driver tab structure. README says the AI portion (205-prefixed) gets its own linked tab; BRP27 embedded it inside the MI portion's sheet instead.

Impact: recursive linking logic needs to support both, or one must be picked.
3

Drill & tap charge placement. Documented rule excludes drill/tap from the Phase-1 total (reference line only); BRP27's manual sheet folded it into the housing total.

Impact: changes the BOM Cost number, not just presentation. Should ship as a configurable flag.
4

SPD assembly %. One product's SPD sheet reused the driver-potted 15% instead of an independent SPD %.

Impact: determines whether Input Sheet fields E and F may ever be assumed equal.
